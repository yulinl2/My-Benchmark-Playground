4.    Soybean yields in a particular region are known to average 43 bushels per acre, with a standard 
deviation of 4 bushels.   A researcher has developed a new variety of soybeans to be used in the 
region, that the researcher claims will increase yields to an average of 46 bushels per acre, with 
the same standard deviation of 4 bushels. 
      The researcher wants to take a random sample of n acres in the region, plant the new variety on 
each acre.  The researcher’s claim will be supported (at the one sided 0.05 level of significance) if 
the sample mean yield is large enough.  The researcher wants the test to have a 90% chance of 
proving her claim (given that it is true). 
      How big should n be?  Given that value of n, how big does the sample mean yield have to be to 
prove her claim.

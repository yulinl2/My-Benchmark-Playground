1. Consider a game of ring toss with the n players and m posts.  Each player has 1 ring and 
randomly chooses a post at which to toss his or her ring.  Each player has probability p of 
hitting the chosen post (and probability 0 of hitting any other post); the player receives 
one point if they hit the post.  The game is over when each of the n players has tossed his 
or her ring. 
(a)  What is the expected number of total points earned by all players in the game? 
(b) What is the expected number of distinct posts hit at the end of the game?

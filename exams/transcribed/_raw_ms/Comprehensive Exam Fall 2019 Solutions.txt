We are interested in comparing the salaries among the 3 samples (RU, UC, PS). 
1. Draw a boxplot of the groups by calculating median, quartiles and extremes of the 
three samples. The p-value of the ANOVA F-test for comparing the means of the 3

cannot detect it.  
 
 
 
2. The Spread Vs level plot has a slope between 1 and 1.5 so the transformation is 
powers 0 to -0.5.

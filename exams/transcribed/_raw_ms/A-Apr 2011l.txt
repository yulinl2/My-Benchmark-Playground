3: There are three coins in a barrel. These coins, when flipped, will come up heads with 
respective probabilities 0.4, 0.5, and 0.9. A coin is randomly selected from among these three 
and is then flipped ten times

b.  Let N be the number of heads obtained on the ten flips. Find P(N=n), where n=0,1,…,10.

each time a tail appears in order to have an expected gain of 0? 
d.  Now the rule is changed. We select one coin each time at random and continue this 
process for 10 times with replacement. What is P(N=n)?

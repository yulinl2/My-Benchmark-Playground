1. Dawn-Holly plants have male and female genders.  The time for a single female Dawn-Holly plant 
grown alone to bloom from when it is born (i.e. emergence from the ground) in weeks is distributed 
exponential with λ=10; f(t) = (1/10) e-t/10.  The time for a single male Dawn-Holly grown alone to 
bloom is exponential with parameter λ=15; (1/15) e-t/15.  However, if a female plant is grown paired 
with (i.e. within 50 feet of) a male plant the time to blooming for both plants is independent except that 
once the male plant blooms, the pollen released by the male triggers the female plant to immediately 
bloom if the female plant did not bloom first. Thus when a male and female Dolly-Holly are paired 
together, the time for the female plant to bloom is min (Y, Z) where Y ~ exponential(λ=10) and Z ~ 
exponential(λ=15). 
 
A. Compared to growing a female Dolly-Holly alone, by how much time on average does 
pairing the female plant with a male Dolly-Holly plant reduce the expected time to bloom 
for a female Dolly-Holly?  
 
B. If N=100 independent pairs of female and male Dolly-Holly plants are grown, Let X be then 
number of pairs where the male Dolly-Holly bloomed first and stimulated the female plant 
to bloom.   What is the probability that X ≥ 50?

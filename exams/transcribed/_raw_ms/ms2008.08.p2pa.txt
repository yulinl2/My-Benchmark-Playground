from all U.S. May college graduates.
(You can assume that the number of graduates is

the sample mean loan outstanding is $2,500 , and the sample standard deviation of loan
outstanding is $700.

standing loans.

dents. (This population includes students without outstanding loans and students with
outstanding loans.)

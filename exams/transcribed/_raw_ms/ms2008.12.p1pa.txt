1. In a class of 30 students, 5 of the students play chess, and the other 25 do not. Four
students are picked at random from the class. Given that at least one of the four students
plays chess, what is the probability that at least two of the four students play chess?

0.4x + 0.1, for 0 ≤x ≤2. Let Y = 3X −4.
a. Find V ar(Y ).
b. Find the probability density function of Y .
3. Let Y have a binomial distribution with 10 trials and success probability π; that is,
Y ∼B(10, π). Test H : π = 1/2 vs. K : π > 1/2. Suppose Y = 9. Find the p-value of a test
for the null hypothesis H vs. the alternative hypothesis K. If the size of the test is α = .05,
do I accept or reject?

 Instructions: Attempt any three out of the following four questions. Mention which three you 
would like to have graded. Open book and open notes. 
1) A bowl on my desk has 5 cashew nuts, 5 walnuts, and 5 peanuts.  I pick nuts at random from the 
bowl, one at a time, and eat them as I pick them. 
a) Given that I have eaten exactly 5 nuts, what is the expected number of cashews left in 
the bowl? 
b) Given I have eaten exactly 5 nuts, what is the probability that I have eaten at least one 
of each type of nut? 
c) Given I have eaten exactly 5 nuts, what is the expected number of different types of 
nuts I have eaten? 
 
2) Let the waiting time until a light bulb fails be exponential, with expected waiting time of 1000 
hours.  Assume that the lifetimes of light bulbs are independent of each other.  Four new 
lightbulbs are put in a room, and all four lights are turned on and kept on. 
a) What is the expected waiting time until at least 2 of the 4 light bulbs fail? 
b) What is the expected number of light bulbs that fail in the first 1000 hours? 
c) What is the probability that in the first 2000 hours that at least 3 of the light bulbs fail? 
 
3)  (a)  Results of my last 8 tennis matches were as follows:  win, lose, lose, lose, lose, lose, lose, 
lose.  Find the exact P-value using a sign test to determine if my probability of winning is the 
same as my probability of losing vs the alternative that these probabilities are different. 
 
(b)  Find the approximate P-value that my probability to win is the same as that to lose if I won 
only 10 matches out of my last 100.

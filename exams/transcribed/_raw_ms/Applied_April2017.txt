pages.  The teacher asks the student to translate all 40 words on each chosen page.  (a total of 
240 words).  The teacher records for each sampled page the number of Spanish words on the 
page for which the student knew the correct English translation.  The following are the number 
of words the student knew correctly for 6 chosen pages:     13,  36,  37,  13,  12,   15 . 
 
a) Test H0:  that the student does not know the correct translation of more than half the words in 
the (200 pages of the) dictionary), against the alternative at the 0.05 level of significance.  Show 
the formula for the test statistic used, and the substitution of numbers in the formula.

translation in the (200 pages of the) dictionary.  Give the standard error of this estimate.  Show
